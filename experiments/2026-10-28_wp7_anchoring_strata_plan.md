# Prespecified plan: WP7 stratified replication of the cited-interval effect on a fresh cohort (written 2026-10-28 01:05 UTC, before any WP7 run)

## Why
Post hoc CPU analysis of the WP2 receipts (experiments/2026-10-28_faithfulness_followups.md, section D) found that the average necessity effect of the cited interval (~0.3 nat) hides two populations: start-anchored citations (group 0 in the cited set; 37% test / 44% val) with small effects (+0.15 nat in both cohorts), and non-anchored citations whose effect is concentrated where the citation overlaps the annotated evidence (+0.69 nat in both cohorts; +0.06..+0.09 without overlap). These strata were defined after seeing the data, so they are exploratory. WP7 replicates the stratified question on a cohort never used for WP2/WP3: the WP1 test cohort (N=112 requested, the exclusion manifest and ranking of the WP1 experiment; its videos were not part of the WP2/WP3/WP4/WP5 cohorts).

## Design (fixed now)
- Runs: WP2 runner (scripts/run_wp2_cited_evidence.py, unchanged, sdpa, 7B) and WP3 runner (scripts/run_wp3_cited_sufficiency.py, unchanged) on the WP1 test cohort (WPROOT/WPEXCL, N from evidence_pathway_knockout_N_20261023_60.txt). Greedy decoding makes the WP2 and WP3 citations identical per video.
- Strata (defined now, identical to the exploratory definition): A = start-anchored (cited groups contain group 0); B1 = not anchored and cited_annotation_overlap > 0; B0 = not anchored and no overlap. Expected sizes at N~105 analysed: A ~40, B1 ~35, B0 ~30.
- Primary 1 (replication of the concentrated effect): WP2 necessity effect (self-margin drop, cited minus sham knockout) in stratum B1 is supported iff t, sign-flip and Wilcoxon p < 0.05 and mean >= 0.3 nat (the same rule as WP2 within the stratum). Primary 2 (the default-citation population): in stratum A the WP2 necessity effect mean < 0.3 nat (descriptive with the 95% interval; "small" iff the upper end of the interval < 0.3). Primary 3: difference of the necessity effect B1 minus A, Welch t p < 0.05 and difference >= 0.3 nat. Holm over the three p-values (t-based). Positive control as in WP2 (whole-video v2t knockout mean relative change <= -20% on baseline-correct videos); if it fails the results are uninformative.
- Secondary (descriptive): the same strata for WP3 sufficiency (keep-cited minus keep-sham, gold margin), the pooled WP2 and WP3 primary effects on this cohort with the original thresholds, the share of start-anchored citations, and the three-cohort agreement of the direction (test/val/WP1 cohort).
- Each analysis once; no pooling with the test or val cohorts; N is small (about 105), so a stratum result that is not supported is reported as "not detectable at this N" unless TOST supports equivalence within +-0.3 nat.
- Workload: ~1 generation + 5 forwards (WP2) and ~4 forwards (WP3) per video x 112, about 40 min each; smoke n=3 first; new tags. Queued behind the 3B replication to avoid three concurrent GPU jobs.

## Limits
Small N; strata are post hoc in origin (this is the point of the replication); one model, one citation format; label-free; measures dependence on the cited interval, not faithfulness; a cohort of 112 cannot establish the size of stratum effects precisely.
