# Faithfulness follow-ups: CPU findings from existing WP2/WP3 receipts and the candidate next experiments (2026-10-28)

## A. Exploratory CPU analyses (no GPU; WP2 and WP3 receipts, same videos matched by media hash, same citation in WP2 and WP3 for 265/265 test and 289/289 val videos; not prespecified, no multiplicity correction)
- **Necessity vs sufficiency per video.** Spearman between the WP2 necessity effect (self-margin drop, cited minus sham knockout) and the WP3 sufficiency effect (gold margin, keep-cited minus keep-sham): test +0.368 [+0.239, +0.491], val +0.366 [+0.243, +0.489] (bootstrap over videos). The two intervention directions agree per video to a moderate degree, in both cohorts: videos whose cited interval matters for removal also tend to be the ones where keeping it preserves the answer. (Different endpoints: self-margin vs gold margin.)
- **Grounding of the citation.** The cited groups overlap the annotated evidence groups in 57% (test) and 58% (val) of videos; mean group-level IoU 0.29 in both. Per-video Spearman(necessity effect, IoU): test +0.121 [-0.004, +0.246], val +0.168 [+0.046, +0.278]. Split by overlap: necessity effect is +0.443 nat (n=150) with any overlap vs +0.129 (n=115) without on test, +0.444 (n=168) vs +0.095 (n=121) on val. Inference: the effect of removing the cited interval is concentrated in videos where the citation falls on the annotated evidence; where it does not, the effect is close to zero.
- Scope: these relate the model's own citation to the dataset annotation (an interval-dependence endpoint); they are not a faithfulness verdict.

## B. Candidate next experiments (not yet implemented)
1. **WP5 input-level necessity/sufficiency of the cited interval (recommended first).** WP2-WP4 intervene on attention masks, an out-of-distribution edit that the reviewers of the base paper and the faithfulness-metric literature criticize (masking OOD, metric disagreement). WP5 changes the input instead: (a) replace the cited frames by copies of the nearest non-cited frames (same frame count and geometry, so token layout and positions are unchanged), versus the same operation on an equal-count sham; (b) keep only the cited frames (replace all others by the nearest cited frame) versus sham. Endpoint: answer margin as in WP2/WP3; the primary question is whether the attention-level result (cited minus sham about 0.3 nat) reproduces at the input level, i.e. agreement across intervention methods. Same cohorts, citation read from the WP2 receipts (deterministic greedy), about 4 forwards per video, ~1-1.5 h per cohort. To be prespecified before implementation.
2. **Citation stability across option order.** Regenerate the citation under the cyclic option-order shifts used in PERM and measure agreement of the cited interval (group IoU) and of the answer; an unstable citation cannot be a faithful explanation. Cost: 4 generations per video, similar to WP4.
3. **Per-video agreement of the attention-level and input-level necessity (follow-up of WP5, CPU).**
4. Not pursued now: free-form explanation text scoring (no label-free scorer; human review is not available), further correction experiments (null on PERM/CEN, CSL only sharpens confidence).
