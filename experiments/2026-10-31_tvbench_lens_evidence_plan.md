# 2026-10-31 WP4-TVB: do the video tokens of the annotated evidence interval decode to the gold action? (Logit Lens on evidence tokens) — prespecified plan

Status: PLAN, written before any WP4 outcome. Runner `scripts/run_tvbench_lens_evidence.py`; analysis `scripts/analyze_lens_evidence.py` (written before the full run).
## Why
Masking and frame removal (WP1-WP3) intervene on the input/attention. This experiment uses NO intervention: it reads the model's own representations of the video tokens, split into the tokens that belong to the annotated evidence interval and the rest, and asks whether the evidence tokens carry the information of the gold option earlier/more than the other tokens (a representation-level, OOD-free check of "the evidence interval contains the answer-relevant action").
## Design
- Qwen2.5-VL-7B (4-bit), TVBench Action Sequence (2 options, e.g. "Put down the blanket." vs "Took the towel."), 32 frames = 16 temporal groups, fp32-free bf16 lens readout (as Logit Lens v2), no gradient.
- Linear (ridge) per-layer translator from the normalised layer-l state to the final normalised state (Logit Lens v2 recipe), trained on 40 TRAIN videos = the first 40 videos of the disjoint batch B (`--skip 200`), evaluated on TEST = the first 100 videos of batch A (`tvb-ev-v1` ranking; videos with an invalid annotation are excluded as before). Train and test are disjoint.
- Target word sets from the option texts: content words (alphabetic, >= 3 letters, stop-words removed, first sub-word of "w", " w", capitalised variants); G' = words of the gold option not in the foil option, F' = words of the foil option not in the gold option (shared words removed). Videos with an empty G' or F' are excluded from the lens metric.
- For each video token t and layer l: d_t(l) = log P_lens(G') - log P_lens(F') where P(S) is the lens probability mass of the id set S (full-vocabulary softmax). Tokens are grouped by temporal group; stored: mean over tokens of log P(G') and of log P(F') per group and layer (so any aggregation is possible offline). Also stored: the last-token gold-minus-foil option margin per layer through the same lens (answer emergence).
## Primary analysis (one; video = unit)
Layers 9-20 (middle band, tuned lens): per video C = mean over evidence groups of d - mean over non-evidence groups of d. Test mean(C) against 0 (paired t, sign-flip, Wilcoxon, bootstrap 95 % CI 10k seed 0), report d_z and the share of videos with C > 0 (Wilson CI, sign test). Hypothesis (directional, stated before running): C > 0. No pass/fail threshold.
Secondary (descriptive): (a) layer profile of C (all 28 layers) with CI per layer; (b) late band 21-28; (c) evidence vs equal-count disjoint hash sham groups (same sham construction as WP1-TVB; fewer videos); (d) evidence not containing group 0; (e) baseline-correct subset; (f) answer emergence: last-token margin per layer.
## Limits
Lens on a 4-bit model with a linear translator (KL to the final distribution remains 1.5-2.5 nats in the middle layers); word sets from two short option texts, first-subword matching; the evidence interval often coincides with the first frames (group 0), tokens in different groups also differ in position and content; tokens interact (cross-frame attention) so "evidence tokens" also carry context from other frames; representation-level reading, not a causal test and not explanation faithfulness.
