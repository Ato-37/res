not. Pustejovsky and Tipton's fixed-effects regression work studies CR2/bias-
reduced linearization and Satterthwaite small-sample tests under a working
model; it is methodological background, not a turnkey recommendation for one
video-level mask contrast ([paper](https://jepusto.com/publications/RVE-in-fixed-effects-models/),

small-cluster approaches in cluster-randomized trials; the setting's
randomization and estimands are not established for these sampled videos
([article record and abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29025158/)).
Hagemann's placebo/randomization framework is built around cluster-level
