## Protocol-readiness audit and repair checkpoint — 11:44 JST

An independent validator audit found that schema v12 accepted an eligible
cohort of one video although the fixed Student-t method uses sample SD and
`df=n-1`, both undefined for `n=1`. The primary validator now requires at
least two eligible videos and emits `analysis_unit_count_below_minimum`; a
regression test reproduces the one-video case. The protocol contract now states
that this is only a mathematical minimum, not a precision or population-
assumption guarantee.

A separate review found no current executable check that applies timing and
pixel-identity declarations atomically across a frozen cohort. Added a
CPU-only, synthetic timing contract helper and tests. The helper recomputes
PTS-derived presentation times and the frozen sampler/evidence/control
assignment, requires a uniform sampled grid within the caller's exact
tolerance, compares frame identity hashes and processor index/FPS metadata,
and checks matching timing declarations across full/selected/control. A
two-video valid fixture passes structurally; injected failures in the final
cohort member (FPS, pixel identity, nonuniform/nonmonotonic PTS, or no evidence)
block the whole denominator, with no scoring path. The helper is deliberately
not wired into the production cross-gate preflight or inference runner; fixture
hashes do not authenticate pixels, decoder provenance, or annotation-clock
calibration. The pre-existing CPU→Qwen fixture audit and production time gate
remain separate, so no real-data execution was enabled.
