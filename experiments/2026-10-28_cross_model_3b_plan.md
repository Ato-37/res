# Prespecified plan: cross-model replication of WP1 and WP2 on Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct (written 2026-10-28, before any 3B run)

## Why
All results so far are for one model (Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct), the main limitation named in the thesis and by the base paper's reviewers. Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct is cached locally (snapshot 66285546d2b821cf421d4f5eb2576359d3770cd3, 36 layers, hidden size 2048; same processor and vision tokenizer, so the video token layout and the temporal groups are identical). Question: does the video->text pathway dependence on the annotated evidence interval (WP1) and on the model's own cited interval (WP2) appear in a smaller sibling model with the band placed at the same relative depth? Label-free; scope = answer margin on NExT-GQA, not explanation faithfulness.

## Fixed now (no tuning)
- Model: 3B, same loading as 7B (4-bit weights, BF16 compute, fp32 lm_head readout, sdpa with explicit masks); selected through the environment variables RES_MODEL_ID / RES_MODEL_REVISION; the 7B defaults are unchanged and the unit tests for the touched modules pass.
- Layer band mapped proportionally from 6-20 of 28 (21%-71% of depth) to **layers 8-26 of 36** (6*36/28=7.7 -> 8; 20*36/28=25.7 -> 26); the all-layer band is 1-36. RES_BAND=8-26, RES_NUM_LAYERS=36. The band is not tuned and not swept; a single deviation (a different band) would need its own prespecification.
- WP1-3B: same runner (scripts/run_evidence_pathway_knockout.py), same cohort (the WP1 test cohort, N=112 requested, same exclusion manifest), same analyzer and rules unchanged (P1 video->text band: t, sign-flip and Wilcoxon p<0.05 and effect >= 0.3 nat, Holm over P1/P2; positive control: whole-video video->text mean relative change <= -20% on baseline-correct videos). If the positive control fails, nulls are uninformative.
- WP2-3B: same runner (scripts/run_wp2_cited_evidence.py), the WP2 test cohort (N=278 requested), same analyzer and rule (mean >= 0.3 nat with t/sign-flip/Wilcoxon p<0.05; positive control as in WP2). The 3B model may format the citation differently or ignore the format; the parse rate and the share of usable citations are reported and a low usable share (<50% of recorded) is reported as "citation format not followed" rather than as an absence of dependence.
- Each analysis once, no pooling with the 7B results; 3B and 7B baselines are separate (baseline accuracy is expected to differ).
- Workload: WP1 about 13 forwards/video x 112, WP2 about 1 generation + 5 forwards x 278; 3B is faster than 7B; smoke n=3 first; new tags.

## Limits
One additional smaller model of the same family (not an independent architecture); the band mapping is a proportional assumption; 3B accuracy may be low, shrinking the baseline-correct subset used by the positive control; label-free endpoint measures dependence on the interval, not faithfulness.

## Implementation note (2026-10-28 00:58 UTC, before any 3B outcome)
The first WP1-3B full run progressed at about 100 s/video because the WP1 runner built four unused masked conditions per video (CPU-bound preprocessing, GPU idle). The runner now calls `prepare_conditions(..., only_full=True)` (the same equivalent speed-up as in the SGC/CSL/CEN/WP2 runners, whose full-condition batch tensors were verified identical on CPU); the WP1 runner keeps eager attention as before. The 3 records written before the restart (same arms, same code semantics) are kept via --resume. No outcome of the 3B run had been inspected.

## Change before any 3B outcome (2026-10-28 02:00 UTC): WP1-3B under sdpa, restarted from scratch
The first WP1-3B full run (eager attention, tag p) wrote 13 records in the first ~25 minutes and then no record for 36 minutes while the 7B WP5 job ran on the same GPU (VRAM 13.5 of 16.3 GB, GPU utilization ~0%, CPU 85%): consistent with VRAM pressure (WSL GPU paging) from eager attention matrices, not with a code error. No outcome of the 13 records was inspected. The WP1 runner now has `--attn` (default eager = the original setting, unchanged for 7B) and lazy frame decoding; WP1-3B is rerun from scratch with `--attn sdpa` under a new tag (s), so eager and sdpa records are not mixed in one receipt; the 13 eager records are discarded unanalysed. WP2-3B already used sdpa. The 3B results are therefore sdpa-based; the 7B WP1 results (eager) are not pooled with them.

## Result: WP1 on Qwen2.5-VL-3B (N=112 recorded, 0 excluded, 74 baseline-correct; band layers 8-26 of 36; sdpa; one prespecified analysis; 2026-10-28 03:07 UTC; receipt wp1m3b_full_20261028s_wp1m3b.json local-only, results/wp1m3b_summary_20261028s_wp1m3b.json)
Positive control passed (whole-video video->text knockout: mean relative change -34.4% on baseline-correct videos). P1 (video->text, band, evidence minus sham knockout, margin change): **-0.255 nat [-0.481, -0.030]**, t p=0.027, sign-flip p=0.026, Wilcoxon p=0.010, TOST(0.3) p=0.35; evidence-vs-baseline -0.176, sham-vs-baseline +0.079. P2 (video->last, band): +0.007 [-0.003, +0.016] (equivalent to zero, TOST p~1e-89). All-layers variants: S1 video->text -0.213 [-0.434, +0.007] (t p=0.058, Wilcoxon p=0.046), S2 video->last -0.002. The prespecified rule for P1 (all three p < 0.05, mean <= -0.3 nat, Holm) is **not met** (mean -0.255, below the 0.3 nat threshold). Comparison with 7B (eager, N=112): P1 -0.68 [Holm p=4e-5], P2 ~0.
Interpretation (inference): in the 3B model the direction of the 7B result replicates (evidence knockout hurts more than the sham knockout on the video->text pathway; the direct video->last pathway is not used; the positive control passes), but the effect is about 0.26 nat, below the prespecified 0.3 nat threshold, so by the rule it is not a replication of the effect size; with a proportionally placed band (8-26) the dependence on the annotated interval is weaker in the smaller model, or the band mapping is suboptimal (no band sweep was planned or run). 3B and 7B settings differ (sdpa vs eager); no pooling. WP2-3B is running.

## Result: WP2 on Qwen2.5-VL-3B (278 recorded, 118 excluded, 160 analysed; sdpa; band 8-26; one prespecified analysis; 2026-10-28 04:46 UTC; results/wp2m3b_summary_20261028s_wp2m3b.json)
Citation format: parse rate 69% of recorded (no time span in the rest), 58% usable (160/278, above the 50% reporting threshold fixed in the plan; the 118 exclusions are unusable citations: none or wider than half the video, plus annotation-mapping failures). Positive control passed. Primary cited minus sham knockout (self-margin drop): **+0.066 nat [-0.096, +0.228]**, t p=0.42, sign-flip p=0.43, Wilcoxon p=0.36, TOST(0.3) p=0.0024 -> **equivalent within delta_min** (7B test: +0.307 nat, supported; 7B val: +0.298). Descriptive: cited groups overlap the annotation in 55.6% of analysed videos (mean overlap 1.40 vs 0.95 by chance); necessity effect with overlap +0.106 (n=89) vs without +0.016 (n=71); the citation letter matches the forced-choice prediction in 80% (7B: ~97%).
Interpretation (inference): the 3B model's citations are less often usable, less grounded than the 7B's (the overlap-conditioned effect is +0.11 vs +0.44-0.69 nat), and removing them changes the margin by less than 0.3 nat (equivalence supported). The 7B finding that the model's own citation matters about 0.3 nat is not reproduced in the smaller model; together with the WP1-3B result (same direction as 7B but 0.26 nat), the dependence on the annotated or cited interval is clearly weaker in 3B. One additional model of the same family, a proportional band assumption, and a selection of analysed videos by citation usability limit the generality of this statement.
