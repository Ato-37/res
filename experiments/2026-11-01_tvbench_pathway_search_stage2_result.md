Setup: `scripts/run_tvbench_pruning_qwenranges.py` (smoke 3 videos OK, then full), `scripts/analyze_pruning_qwenranges.py` (run once; derived `results/tvbench_pruning_qwenranges_summary_20261101.json`). Qwen-derived ranges fixed before the run from stage 1 by the rule "union of 5-layer windows with effect >= half of the edge's strongest": cross_frame 5-17, video->text 15-19, text->last 15-21 + 23-27 (1-based; video->last blocked in all layers). ## Interpretation (not observation)
The Qwen-derived ranges did not outperform the LLaVA-scaled ranges: lower accuracy than baseline (significant), no clear gain over its matched random control, and no clear difference from the scaled arm. So single-edge knockout windows (stage 1) do not translate into a better "keep-only" pathway; the stage-1 rule gave a narrower pathway (38 % blocked vs 45 %), and random control accuracy is itself higher in this arm (less blocked), which makes the margin over random smaller. The scaled-range advantage over random is the previously reported result and replicates here on this cohort (+11.9). moving_direction drops for both pathway arms (below its random control for Qwen), a task-specific weakness. Limits: not matched in blocked share across arms; the Qwen ranges come from a 40-video, single-task selection set (Action Sequence), not the other three tasks; single run, exploratory; no multiplicity correction; 4-bit model; rule fixed once, no tuning of the rule after seeing results. Not explanation faithfulness.
